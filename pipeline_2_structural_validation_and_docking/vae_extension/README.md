# VAE Extension — Latent-Space Counterfactual Peptide Optimization

## Overview

This directory contains an experimental **Variational Autoencoder (VAE) extension** of the PEARL Pipeline 2 CLEAR-based peptide optimization workflow.

The main Pipeline 2 implements a constrained, interpretable counterfactual search directly in peptide sequence / categorical space.

This extension investigates an alternative strategy:

```text
peptide sequence
      ↓
VAE encoder
      ↓
continuous latent representation z
      ↓
counterfactual optimization in latent space
      ↓
VAE decoder
      ↓
candidate peptide sequence
      ↓
CLEAR constraints + peptide oracle
```

The purpose of this branch is not to replace the consolidated Direct CLEAR workflow, but to test whether a learned continuous latent representation can provide an additional and independent route toward local peptide counterfactuals.

---

## Relationship with Pipeline 2

The VAE extension depends on outputs generated by the main CLEAR branch of Pipeline 2.

```text
04c_CLEAR_Local_Peptide_Variant_Dataset
        ↓
04d_CLEAR_Peptide_Oracle_Training
        │
        ├──────────────→ 04e Direct CLEAR
        │
        └──────────────→ VAE extension
                           ↓
                         06a
                           ↓
                         06b
                           ↓
                         06c
                           ↓
                         06d
```

The reference peptide is:

```text
F0010
IGERCQYRDLK
```

The VAE is trained on the local peptide-variant dataset generated in Notebook `04c`, while the counterfactual optimization uses the peptide oracle trained in Notebook `04d`.

# Notebooks

## `06a_CLEAR_Peptide_VAE_Training.ipynb`

Trains a true Variational Autoencoder on the local peptide sequence landscape generated around F0010.

The architecture includes:

```text
peptide sequence
      ↓
amino-acid embedding
      ↓
encoder
      ↓
μ, logσ²
      ↓
reparameterization
      ↓
latent vector z
      ↓
decoder
      ↓
categorical amino-acid probabilities
```

The VAE objective combines:

- sequence reconstruction loss;
- KL-divergence regularization.

Main diagnostics include:

- residue-level reconstruction accuracy;
- exact sequence reconstruction;
- reconstruction of F0010;
- KL-divergence;
- latent-space variance;
- local decoding around the F0010 latent representation.

In the current experiment:

- the seed F0010 is reconstructed exactly;
- the latent space does not show posterior collapse;
- local latent perturbations generate peptides close to F0010;
- test residue reconstruction accuracy is lower than the initial conservative 90% threshold.

The model is therefore treated as a useful local latent representation rather than a globally validated peptide generative model.

---

## `06b_CLEAR_VAE_Latent_Space_Analysis.ipynb`

Evaluates whether the learned VAE latent space is sufficiently structured to support counterfactual optimization.

The notebook examines:

- Euclidean latent distance versus Hamming sequence distance;
- organization of seed, single mutants and double mutants;
- PCA projections of the latent space;
- relationship between latent variables and the local 04c target;
- local target smoothness;
- nearest latent neighbors of F0010;
- location of previously identified CLEAR candidates;
- a simple latent-space direction associated with target improvement.

The current VAE passed all diagnostic readiness checks:

```text
READY_FOR_06C
5 / 5 checks passed
```

The analysis showed that latent distance is moderately related to sequence distance and that the local oracle target retains measurable structure in the latent representation.

## `06c_CLEAR_VAE_Latent_Counterfactual_Optimization.ipynb`

Performs the actual counterfactual search in the learned VAE latent space.

Starting from:

```text
z_seed = Encoder(F0010)
```

the latent vector is optimized while keeping both the VAE and the peptide oracle frozen.

Conceptually:

```text
z_seed
  ↓
optimize z
  ↓
VAE decoder
  ↓
soft amino-acid probabilities
  ↓
04d peptide oracle
  ↓
gradient backpropagation to z
```

CLEAR-style constraints are enforced during decoding:

- maximum 1–2 amino-acid substitutions;
- protected positions remain unchanged;
- only substitutions allowed by the 04c positional constraints are accepted;
- latent distance from F0010 is bounded;
- final candidates must improve the oracle relative to F0010.

The current VAE-CLEAR search produced four successful counterfactual sequences:

```text
IGERSQYRELK   C5S + D9E
IGERCQYRELK   D9E
IGERCQYREMK   D9E + L10M
IGERSQYRDLK   C5S
```

The best VAE-CLEAR candidate was:

```text
IGERSQYRELK
C5S + D9E
```

with an oracle improvement of approximately:

```text
+0.0958
```

relative to F0010.

# Cross-Method Interpretation

The VAE extension did not identify peptide sequences outside the candidate space already discovered by Direct CLEAR.

Its main contribution is therefore **algorithmic convergence rather than expansion of the candidate space**.

In particular:

```text
Direct CLEAR
      │
      ├───────────────┐
      │               ↓
      │           C5S + D9E
      │               ↑
      └──────── VAE-CLEAR
```

Two different search strategies converge on the same high-priority local counterfactual.

This strengthens the computational prioritization of the mutation pattern but should not be interpreted as independent biological validation, because both approaches ultimately rely on the same local peptide dataset and peptide oracle.

# Integration with Structural Validation

Some shared Direct/VAE candidates had already undergone explicit structural evaluation in the main Pipeline 2.

For example:

```text
C5S + D9E
IGERSQYRELK
```

showed:

- strong oracle improvement;
- favorable FoldX ΔΔG;
- preserved structural contacts;
- a reasonable FlexPepDock profile;
- favorable adjacent-pair surrogate score.

In contrast:

```text
D9E
IGERCQYRELK
```

was favored by the oracle and FoldX but performed less favorably in the final FlexPepDock ranking.

This suggests that D9E is an important local mutation, but its structural effect depends on the surrounding mutational context.

# Methodological Interpretation

The two optimization strategies should be distinguished clearly.

## Direct CLEAR

```text
F0010
↓
categorical amino-acid representation
↓
differentiable peptide oracle
↓
direct constrained optimization
↓
counterfactual sequence
```

## VAE-CLEAR

```text
F0010
↓
VAE encoder
↓
latent representation z
↓
latent-space optimization
↓
VAE decoder
↓
counterfactual sequence
```

The VAE extension therefore implements a true learned latent representation and a true counterfactual search in latent space.

---

# Limitations

The VAE extension should be interpreted as a local methodological experiment.

Important limitations include:

- the VAE is trained on only 539 local peptide variants;
- most training sequences differ from F0010 by only one or two substitutions;
- the learned latent space therefore represents a highly local peptide landscape;
- the VAE did not generate successful sequences outside the existing 04c sequence set;
- the peptide oracle is itself a surrogate trained on the same local dataset;
- agreement between Direct CLEAR and VAE-CLEAR is algorithmic convergence, not independent biological evidence;
- adjacent-pair scoring is also derived from the local 04c target;
- FoldX and Rosetta provide computational structural evidence but not experimental binding affinity;
- experimental validation is required before any candidate can be considered a confirmed binder or inhibitor.

---

# Main Conclusion

The VAE extension demonstrates that a continuous learned peptide latent space can support constrained counterfactual optimization around F0010.

The latent-space search independently recovers several of the strongest Direct CLEAR solutions, including:

```text
C5S + D9E
D9E
```

In the current local landscape, the VAE does not expand the candidate space beyond Direct CLEAR.

Its main value is therefore:

```text
independent search strategy
        +
cross-method convergence
        +
latent-space representation
```

rather than discovery of previously unseen peptide candidates.

The resulting candidates remain **computationally prioritized hypotheses** requiring structural and ultimately experimental validation.
